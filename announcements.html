Gentle Warrior 1st Anniversary — From Awareness to Action

November 29, 2026

As we prepare for the Gentle Warrior's 1st anniversary, we are taking another step toward turning cerebral palsy awareness into meaningful action.

In collaboration with Hong’s Children Center for Cerebral Palsy, our goal is to support:

20 CHILDREN WITH CEREBRAL PALSY
4 FREE THERAPY SESSIONS EACH
80 therapy sessions in total

What began as one family’s journey is growing into a community effort to help more children and families.
