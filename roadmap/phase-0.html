PHASE 0

Foundation

Establish who Gentle Warrior is, why it exists, where the community lives, and how the project operates transparently.

What is Gentle Warrior?

Gentle Warrior is a world created to raise awareness for children with cerebral palsy.

Soft Heart. Strong Spirit.
Gentle Warrior represents children with cerebral palsy — gentle in body, strong in spirit, and surrounded by care.

Why was it created?

Gentle Warrior was created to give children with cerebral palsy a hero who looks like effort — not perfection.

Cerebral palsy affects movement, coordination, and muscle control. For many children, everyday actions take energy and focus that others never see.

You are not broken.
You are not behind.
You are not invisible.

You are moving through your own path — and that path matters.

The story behind the project

Gentle Warrior was born from a real family's journey through cerebral palsy, therapy, patience, resilience, and hope.

This world exists to honor resilience, patience, and quiet courage through storytelling, symbols, and play.

GENTLE WARRIOR CHARACTER

The Heart of This World

Baby Leam — our very own Gentle Warrior.

From his earliest days to his therapy sessions, every moment you see here is real.

He is the inspiration, the heart, and the reason this project exists.

Mission

To raise awareness for children with cerebral palsy, share real stories of resilience, and create meaningful experiences that encourage understanding, kindness, and support.

Gentle Warrior aims to turn awareness into connection — helping families, caregivers, and communities feel seen, understood, and supported.

Vision

A world where children with cerebral palsy are seen beyond their limitations — recognized for their strength, individuality, potential, and humanity.

Gentle Warrior envisions a growing community where awareness leads to empathy, empathy leads to action, and every little win is worth celebrating.

Soft Heart. Strong Spirit.

Every child deserves to be seen, understood, and valued.
