Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

Welcome to Little Wins. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Little Wins collects, uses, stores, protects, and discloses your personal information when you use our website and services. By creating an account or using Little Wins, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Google account information (when you choose Google Sign-In)
  • Profile photo (if available through your Google account)
  • Journey information such as journey name, community, and journey start date
  • Journal entries, milestones, notes, photos, and other content you voluntarily upload
  • Browser type, operating system, device information, and basic usage analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate your identity.
  • Save and synchronize your Little Wins journey.
  • Display your milestones and journal within your account.
  • Improve the platform and user experience.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Maintain platform security.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Your Journey Data

Your milestones, journal entries, photos, and other journey information belong to you. Little Wins securely stores this information so you can continue your journey across your devices. We do not claim ownership of the content you create.

4. Data Storage and Security

Little Wins uses industry-standard security measures to help protect your personal information and journey data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction. Authentication is handled using secure authentication providers, and sensitive credentials are never stored in plain text.

5. Sharing of Information

Little Wins does not sell or rent your personal information. We may share information only with trusted service providers that help operate the platform, or when disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful government request.

6. Cookies and Analytics

Little Wins may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, keep you signed in, improve user experience, maintain secure sessions, and analyze platform usage.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws, you may request to:

  • Access your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Update your account information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal data, where permitted by law.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.

8. Children's Privacy

Little Wins may be used by parents, guardians, or caregivers to document the journey of a child or another person under their care. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the appropriate authority or consent before uploading information, photos, or milestones relating to another individual.

9. Data Privacy Act of 2012

Little Wins is committed to protecting personal information in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines and follows reasonable practices to safeguard personal data.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Any updates become effective immediately upon publication on this page.
